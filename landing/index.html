MADAD is a website that gathers what pharmacies report about medicine availability, with the time of every report. Search, compare, then call and go. Dispensing happens at the pharmacy.

In Gaza, finding a chronic medicine like insulin means walking from one pharmacy to the next without knowing if it is there. MADAD shortens the route before you take it.
